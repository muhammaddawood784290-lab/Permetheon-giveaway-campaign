# Legal Content Checklist

Status: Needs legal review

This is a preparation checklist, not legal advice.

- [ ] Official Giveaway Rules completed and reviewed.
- [ ] Eligibility and geographic restrictions confirmed.
- [ ] Campaign start, close, and announcement dates confirmed.
- [ ] Prize description and exclusions confirmed.
- [ ] Winner selection and notification process confirmed.
- [ ] No-purchase language reviewed.
- [ ] Privacy Policy completed and reviewed.
- [ ] Cookie Policy completed if cookies or tracking are used.
- [ ] Terms of Use completed and reviewed.
- [ ] Consent text matches actual data use.
- [ ] Marketing consent is separate and optional.
- [ ] Support and contact details are real.
- [ ] Rules and privacy URLs are live before launch.
- [ ] State-specific sweepstakes requirements checked by qualified counsel.
