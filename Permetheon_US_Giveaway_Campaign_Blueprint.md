# Permetheon US Website Giveaway Campaign Blueprint

Implementation-ready plan for a 30-day United States campaign.

## Purpose

This blueprint operationalizes Permetheon’s website giveaway for brand awareness, lead generation, and client acquisition. Company-specific legal entity details, dates, URLs, contacts, prize valuation, and media spend remain placeholders until confirmed.

## Confirmed decisions

- United States only.
- Official Permetheon branding.
- Target audience: existing businesses, startups, and entrepreneurs.
- 30-day campaign.
- Target: 500 completed eligible registrations.
- One grand-prize winner selected by eligibility screening followed by a documented random draw from eligible finalists.
- Prize: responsive business website plus basic on-page SEO, up to 5–7 agreed pages.
- Winner pays domain, hosting, and approved third-party costs.
- Non-winners may receive personalized offers across Permetheon services.
- No budget-range field in the lead-collection system.

## Recommendations and legal items requiring review

Counsel should approve the official rules, US state eligibility and exclusions, registration/bonding requirements, tax treatment, privacy notice, email/SMS and cookie practices, advertising disclosures, minors restrictions, winner publicity, alternate-winner process, and any platform promotion requirements. Marketing consent must not be required to enter. Non-winner data must not be used for marketing without appropriate consent or another reviewed lawful basis.

## Campaign positioning and scope

Recommended positioning: “Give your business a stronger digital foundation.” Permetheon should be presented as a practical growth partner for businesses that need a better website, stronger digital operations, or a clearer path to growth. The giveaway is a qualified-entry promotion, not a guarantee of business results.

Included: responsive website design and build; up to 5–7 agreed pages; discovery; page map; agreed content handoff; QA; launch handoff; and basic on-page SEO such as titles, meta descriptions, headings, alt text, and basic indexability setup.

Excluded unless separately approved: domain, hosting, premium plugins, media licenses, apps, unlimited pages, custom portals, marketplaces, complex integrations, custom software, guaranteed rankings, ongoing SEO, paid media, backlinks, and unlimited revisions.

## Landing page and registration

Use one mobile-first campaign URL. Put the entry CTA above the fold, then explain the prize, exclusions, US eligibility, deadline, official rules, privacy notice, and support route.

Recommended fields: full name, business/project name, email, US state, business stage, primary goal, concise project description, optional website URL, optional services of interest, and official-rules acceptance. Use a separate unticked marketing-consent checkbox. Do not include budget range or infer it from another field.

## Consent, privacy, and data safeguards

- Keep entry acceptance separate from promotional consent; store timestamp, policy/rules version, source URL, consent text/version, and selected channels.
- Collect only what is needed for entry, eligibility, support, segmentation, and permitted follow-up.
- Use role-based CRM access, audit logs, suppression lists, unsubscribe handling, and a documented retention schedule.
- Do not sell or share entrant data for unrelated purposes.
- Do not use non-winner data for marketing without appropriate consent or another reviewed lawful basis.
- Publish legal-entity details, rights process, retention/deletion approach, and processor disclosures once confirmed.

## Eligibility and hybrid winner selection

Recommended eligibility: legal US residents at least 18 or the age of majority, subject to official rules and excluded jurisdictions. Entries must be complete, truthful, non-duplicate, and submitted during the campaign. Exclude employees, contractors, campaign vendors, and immediate household members where counsel recommends.

1. Lock the entry file after close; preserve unique ID, timestamp, source, and consent fields.
2. Screen eligibility using a two-person checklist.
3. Record every eligible finalist and every exclusion reason.
4. Run an auditable random draw; record list identifier, date/time, operator, method, and witness.
5. Verify and notify the selected entrant.
6. Document failed contact or decline and draw an alternate as the rules permit.
7. Announce only after acceptance and any required consent.

## Lead scoring and personalized offers

Use fit plus engagement signals, independent of budget:

| Signal | Example |
|---|---|
| Business stage | Existing business +3; startup/entrepreneur +2 |
| Need clarity | Clear service need +3 |
| Timeline | Actionable timeline +3 |
| Decision access | Founder/owner/decision-maker +2 |
| Engagement | Confirmation/resource engagement +1–2 |
| Consent | Determines permitted channels; never affects eligibility |

Tier A (8+): human follow-up within one business day. Tier B (5–7): personalized follow-up within three business days. Tier C (0–4): useful educational sequence only where permitted. Relevant offers may include web development, e-commerce, custom software, CRM, SEO, digital marketing, and branding.

## Marketing channels, content, and ads

- Website: single source of truth for page, rules, privacy, FAQ, and form.
- LinkedIn: founder and business-growth posts, practical website checklist, partner amplification.
- Instagram/Facebook: explainers, countdowns, and FAQ content compliant with platform rules.
- Email: invitations, reminders, and final-hours messages only to permitted lists.
- Partners: approved announcement kit with no implied endorsement or guaranteed selection.
- Paid media: small test cells; monitor cost per eligible participant, lead quality, frequency, and platform compliance.

Creative set: launch video or motion graphic, three static variants, two educational posts, two FAQ posts, final countdown, and partner kit.

## Budget assumptions

No final budget is confirmed. Create an approval sheet for paid media, creative, landing-page/CRM work, legal review, prize delivery contingency, and measurement. Set spend caps and stop-loss rules after baseline data. Keep the lead form free of budget-range questions.

## KPIs and dashboard

Track reach, landing sessions, completed entries, eligibility rate, conversion rate, cost per eligible participant, qualified-lead rate, tier mix, contact rate, booked calls, proposals, wins, pipeline value, form errors, response time, unsubscribe/complaint rate, winner acceptance, delivery milestones, and satisfaction. Goal: 500 eligible participants. Review daily during the first and final weeks, then at least every two days.

## Roles

Campaign lead: timeline, approvals, issue log, go/no-go. Legal/privacy reviewer: rules, consent, privacy, eligibility, jurisdiction controls. Web/marketing ops: page, form, tracking, integrations, QA, exports, access. Sales owner: tiered follow-up and suppression. Reviewer/witness: screening and draw evidence. Delivery lead: winner scope and website delivery. Support owner: questions and escalation.

## Risk register

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Promotion-law noncompliance | Counsel review; no launch before approval |
| Consent misuse | Separate consent, versioned records, suppression process |
| Fraud or duplicates | Rate limits, CAPTCHA/equivalent, duplicate review, locked export |
| Prize scope dispute | Plain exclusions, signed scope, change control |
| Accessibility/form failure | Keyboard/mobile QA, readable labels, error handling |
| Data breach or over-retention | Least privilege, secure CRM, retention schedule, incident plan |
| Winner unreachable | Permitted contact attempts and documented alternate process |

## Launch checklist and milestones

- Confirm sponsor/legal entity, dates, time zone, US eligibility, exclusions, age rule, official rules, and privacy notice.
- Approve prize scope, page limit, SEO definition, winner-paid costs, content responsibilities, acceptance criteria, and timeline.
- Build and test the form without budget range; verify tracking, confirmation, CRM routing, suppression, accessibility, and mobile behavior.
- Prepare creative, FAQ, support macros, partner kit, dashboard, and escalation contacts.
- Run a mock entry, screening, random draw, notification, and alternate workflow.
- Obtain written go/no-go approval from campaign, legal/privacy, web ops, and sales owners.
- Post-launch: lock entry evidence, document winner, deliver prize, nurture non-winners lawfully, and complete performance review.

Open items: legal entity, campaign URL/contact, exact dates, rules/exclusions, privacy/retention, brand assets, delivery timeline, media cap, CRM, and named owners.

This blueprint is an operational plan, not legal advice. Jurisdiction-specific counsel should approve the rules, privacy and consent language, eligibility, promotion mechanics, advertising disclosures, and tax/reporting treatment before publication.
